              Score = Beat Alignment Score: how close each dance hit lands to a beat or an "and" (100% means every hit is on one; the ms figure is the typical miss). Aligning nudges each move's speed between hits so they land there, favouring the 1 of the bar. The off-beat check takes the best version and moves the song a quarter beat, right between the targets; it should score clearly lower, otherwise the score isn't telling you much.
