          Beat cues

          Ball: over the video, the left ball lands on each hit of the riddim (big amber ring for a kick or bass hit, white for a snare, clap or rim) and the right ball lands on each of the dancer's hits (it appears once they're found: Find hits in the Clip tab, or body tracking). Watch it fall to see the hit coming. When both land together, a line joins them. Count shows 1 2 3 4 under the video. When the app isn't sure, the cues go dim with a "?".

          Sound delay0 ms

          Bluetooth headphones and speakers play sound later than the screen shows it (often 150–250 ms). This shifts the ball and the dancer video to match what you hear. Check it: a click plays and the ball should land on it; move the slider until it does.

              ±20%

              Score = Beat Alignment Score: how close each dance hit lands to a beat or an "and" (100% means every hit is on one; the ms figure is the typical miss). Aligning nudges each move's speed between hits so they land there, favouring the 1 of the bar. The off-beat check takes the best version and moves the song a quarter beat, right between the targets; it should score clearly lower, otherwise the score isn't telling you much.
